Pip Assistant privacy information

Pip is operated for personal use by its owner. It accesses only accounts and Google permissions authorized by that owner. Depending on the granted scopes and requested workflow, data may include email messages and metadata, calendar events, and files.

The assistant uses that data to perform the owner's requested workflows. Authorized content may be processed by the configured AI provider and by Google services as needed to complete those workflows. OAuth credentials and private operational state are stored in the owner's local application environment. This public information site does not receive or store Google account credentials or mailbox contents.

The owner can revoke Pip's Google access in Google Account connections and manage retained local data in the assistant's environment. Revoking access prevents further authenticated Google requests; it does not by itself erase records already retained locally. This page offers no public registration or third-party data-sharing service.

Google data is not sold or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

For questions about this private installation, contact its operator directly. Do not send credentials, verification codes or private account content to this public site.
