Pip Assistant

Pip is a privately operated personal assistant for its owner's use. It connects the owner's Google mail, calendars and files to authorized personal workflows, such as inbox review, draft preparation and reminders. This is not a public signup service. Other people cannot request access through this page.

Pip uses Google OAuth to request the permissions its owner approves. The owner can review and revoke those permissions in Google Account connections. Email sending requires the owner's approval of the exact outgoing message.
